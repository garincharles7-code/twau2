Fabletown. A place where fairy tales hide behind human faces, and every story has a darker side. Old allies, unfamiliar faces, and dangerous enemies cross paths as secrets unravel and trouble brews in the shadows. In this city, nobody is quite as innocent as they seem.

>

Bigby Wolf

The Sheriff. The Big Bad Wolf.

Fabletown's reluctant protector. He has spent years trying to prove he's more than the monster in the stories. Now, with a new conspiracy threatening the city, Bigby must decide whether his instincts will save Fabletown—or destroy what's left of it.

Snow White

The Deputy Mayor.

Keeping Fabletown together is a thankless job. With old tensions resurfacing and new dangers creeping into the city, Snow White has to hold the line—even when the sheriff she depends on becomes part of the problem.

Collin

The Pig Who Won't Leave.

He's seen Bigby at his worst and never been shy about saying what he thinks. Colin is one of the few residents who can speak to the sheriff without fear or flattery. In a town full of secrets, a blunt friend might be worth more than a dozen allies.

Faye Young

The Outsider.

A sharp-minded detective navigating a city full of secrets she was never meant to uncover. Practical, observant, and determined to find the truth, Faye turns to Bigby Wolf for help with a case that may be stranger than anything she's faced before. In a world where fairy tales hide among ordinary people, the truth can be harder to believe than a lie.

Dorothy Gale

The Girl from Oz.

Not every fairy tale ends with a happily ever after. Sitting calmly amid the chaos, Dorothy seems almost unfazed by the trouble unfolding around her. But in a city where appearances deceive, a little indifference might hide a bigger story.

The Scarecrow

The Mind Made for Trouble.

Once a figure from a familiar children's tale, the Scarecrow now finds himself in a world far removed from the Yellow Brick Road. Whatever brought him to Bigby's doorstep, he doesn't look like someone who's come to make friends.

The Tin Man

Cold Steel, Unfinished Business.

Beneath the metal exterior lies a figure caught in a story that has taken a darker turn. Standing alongside the Scarecrow, the Tin Man brings an unmistakable sense of menace to the encounter. Whatever he's after, Bigby may be standing in his way.
